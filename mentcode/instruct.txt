- Camera views:
    - 1: Isometric
    - 2: Top
    - 3: Front
    - 4: Side
- X-ray button: Toggle the semi-transparent cube view.

Timer:
- Space starts inspection.
- Space during inspection starts solving immediately.
- During inspection, the first non-rotation move starts solving.
- Cube rotations x, y, and z are free during inspection.
- During a solve, Space can stop the timer manually after at least one move.
- The timer also stops when the cube is solved.
- Clear resets the timer; it does not reset the cube.

CFOP:
- Run plays a valid case algorithm.
- Setup plays the inverse algorithm to create the case.
- Invalid algorithms are rejected and shown as errors.
